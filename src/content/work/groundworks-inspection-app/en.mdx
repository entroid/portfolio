Groundworks is a mobile app for inspectors to generate extremely detailed records of property conditions in
the field. The work came in as requirements already defined and prioritized by the product
team. My role was to take them and resolve them through interaction patterns and
UI design for complex flows.
Several interesting design problems came out throughout the project: the app had to work
fully offline and sync afterward without the user losing data captured; complex forms had to adapt dynamically based on previous selections (dependent, non-linear data entry); photo capture of key spaces and details had to be integrated directly into the inspection flow; and all of this had to
run with solid performance on mid/low-end devices of the time, within
technical constraints of Xamarin as the development stack and heavy legal government compliance requirements.

## Results

With this project I started to really dig into the basics of UI/UX design for complex workflow applications, with Adobe XD styleguides and rough components. The result was a working field-inspection app, used consistently by the Groundworks contractor inspectors for several years even until today, with continuous targeted iterations post-launch and improvements in the design system, as new use cases and requirements came up.
