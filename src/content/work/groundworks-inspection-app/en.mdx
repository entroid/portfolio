Groundworks is a mobile app for inspectors to record property conditions in
the field. The work came in as requirements already defined by the product
team, and my role was to take them and resolve them through interaction and
UI design for complex flows, rather than leading research from scratch.
Several interesting design problems came out of that: the app had to work
fully offline and sync afterward, without the user losing data captured in
the field; forms had to adapt dynamically based on previous selections
(dependent, non-linear data entry); photo capture of spaces and details had
to be integrated directly into the inspection flow; and all of this had to
run with solid performance on mid/low-end devices of the time, within 
technical constraints of Xamarin as the development stack.

This project's focus was resolving interaction and UI for concrete
technical problems (offline, sync, dynamic forms, camera, performance), not
an extensive research process — and it's presented that way here, without
adding discovery phases that weren't actually part of the work.

## Results

The result was a working field-inspection app, used consistently by the OZ
team for over a year, with targeted iterations post-launch as new use cases
came up.
