Lumen CRM's pipeline view had grown a stage-change flow that took four
clicks and a full-page navigation for something salespeople did dozens of
times a day. This pass reworked it into an inline drag interaction with
optimistic updates, plus a compact detail panel that opens alongside the
board instead of replacing it — so context from the board (who else is in
this stage, how long deals typically sit here) stays visible while editing
a single deal.

The extended scope stayed intentionally narrow: this was a focused
interaction fix within an existing product, not a full redesign, and the
result reflects that — no invented process narrative or metrics beyond what
the change actually touched.
