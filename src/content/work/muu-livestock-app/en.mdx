context: "A scoped redesign of an existing livestock marketplace app, not a from-scratch build."

Muu wanted to modernize its mobile platform for a very specific market: the
buying and selling of livestock. The main challenge was transforming slow,
ambiguous workflows that were frustrating users. I led the project to
completely redefine the UX/UI, focusing on two fronts: simplifying and
speeding up key processes (like publishing a batch of animals or managing
offers), and defining a modern, clean, trustworthy visual identity that
conveyed professionalism to users in the agricultural sector, improving
legibility in outdoor environments (field).

The scope stayed intentionally narrow: this was a UX/UI redesign on an
existing product, not a full rebuild — the result reflects that, without
adding process detail beyond what the change actually touched.

## Results

The result was an application with a much clearer interface, which
accelerated user decision-making and increased confidence in the platform.
