Muu stakeholders wanted to update their v1 mobile platform for a very specific market: the
buying and selling of livestock. The main challenge was transforming unintuitive,
ambiguous workflows that were frustrating users, as well as to solve several usability issues. I led the project to completely redefine the UX/UI and design look & feel, focusing on two fronts: simplifying and
speeding up key processes (like publishing a batch of animals or managing
offers), and defining a modern, clean, trustworthy visual identity that
conveyed professionalism to users in the agricultural sector, improving
legibility in outdoor environments.

The scope stayed intentionally narrow: this was a UX/UI redesign on an
existing product, not a full rebuild. There was little further research as the business already got feedback
from users and the requirements were clear: improve usability, speed, and visual coherence
while keeping the core existing functionality. New features were added as well, like the onboarding flow to guide new users, to improve the overall experience.

## Results

The result was an application with a much clearer, outdoor friendly interface, which
accelerated user decision-making and increased confidence in the platform.
