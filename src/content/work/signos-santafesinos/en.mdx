The project aimed to digitally present a trilogy of historical books, each with its own section:
"Florian Paucke — Hacia allá y para acá," covering the work of an
18th-century Jesuit missionary among the Mocoví people in the region; "Signos Santafesinos en
el Bicentenario," a timeline of key milestones in the province's history; and "Santa Fe entre dos
siglos," a photo gallery spanning 1860 to 1910. The project came with
concrete constraints: government bureaucracy and strict image-usage and content compliance rules
(full resolution, no retouching of any kind), high accessibility priorization and a tight deadline tied to the books' launch date. I built the site as pure
frontend (no database), with HTML, CSS, and jQuery + Bootstrap, with text
and image content for each screen driven by JSON.

The challenge here wasn't interaction complexity, but constraint: limited
tools, limited time, accessibility prioritization, strict image-usage rules, and full ownership of the
entire cycle — discovery, design, code, and deploy — done solo. In addition, each book had its own unique navigation options, interaction design and content structure.

## Results

The site shipped on time for the trilogy's official presentation, meeting
the provincial government's required image-usage and accessibility standards.
