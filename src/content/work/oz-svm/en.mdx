## Approach

We started from scratch, running workshops with stakeholders and future users to understand business needs and current process pain points. From there we organized basic user journeys and core feature requirements, followed by the first versions of information architecture, feature relationships, and user flows — all mapped collaboratively in Mural. The project moved fast, so we prioritized iterating ideas, presenting, and testing hypotheses quickly over polish.

## Key Product Decisions

**• Scoped the POC to 3 core features** (Impact Comparison Tool, Prioritization Matrix, Data Input Flow), leaving dashboard, analytics, and user management for a future phase — necessary scope control for a fast-moving project.

**• Defined 4 user roles upfront** (Decision Makers, Project Owners, Analysts/Contributors, Admin Users), shaping permission and flow decisions from early design rather than as a later patch.

**• Replaced static spreadsheet comparison with a visual, data-driven model** — a bubble chart comparing projects by cost, effort, and strategic impact — directly addressing the root problem: lost strategic clarity in Excel.

## Notes

- **• Collaborative synthesis in Mural:** stakeholder interviews and workshops were translated into information architecture, brainmaps, and user journeys visually and collaboratively, before touching wireframes.

- **• Wireframes as an exploration tool, not an approval gate:** used to explore layout and data-entry flows, with frequent validation sessions alongside stakeholders and potential users — iterating fast rather than arriving with a locked proposal.

- **• Multi-tenant by design:** "My Workspaces" accounted for multiple clients/organizations (including disabled workspace states) as part of the data model from early stages, anticipating the product's use as a service for third parties.

## Results

Early MVP feedback showed faster, more aligned, and data-informed decision-making. Stakeholders requested additional features for the next phase — a signal of strong engagement and perceived value, not just fulfillment of the initial scope.
